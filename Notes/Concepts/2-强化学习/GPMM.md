# GPMM

## 定义
Mixture of Gaussian processes for modeling multi-modal dynamics in world models

## 数学形式
$$p(s_{t+1}|s_t, a_t) = \sum_k \pi_k \mathcal{GP}_k(s_{t+1})$$
